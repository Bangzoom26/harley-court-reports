What This Means for Your Business
Lines Are No Longer the Top Concern
For the first time since the quiz launched, lines and wrinkles have dropped to third place. Contouring and volume loss are now joint top — each with 14 interactions. Karen, this is a meaningful shift. Your audience is increasingly thinking about facial structure and volume, not just surface lines. It may be worth reflecting this in your content over the coming weeks.
Your Most Booking-Ready Month Yet
13 visitors said they are ready to book this month — the highest figure across all three reports and almost double last month's 6. Combined with 7 wanting a consultation first, that's 20 out of your completers actively considering an appointment. The quiz is warming people up effectively, Karen.
First-Timers Are Back in Numbers
New-to-treatments visitors bounced back strongly this month to 12, matching April's figure after a quieter May. This suggests a healthy and consistent flow of brand new prospects finding Harley Court — not just existing clients re-engaging. Your new client pipeline is very much open.
The 50s+ Audience Keeps Growing
Your over-50s audience has increased every single month — from 8, to 13, to 16. This is now clearly your core demographic, and it's growing. If you haven't already tailored any of your website content or social posts specifically to this age group, the data now makes a strong case for doing so.
A note on the figures: Google Analytics records every individual interaction with the quiz, so if a visitor engaged across multiple sessions or revisited questions, each tap registers separately. The impressions figure (252) reflects how many times the quiz was displayed on your site this month — this is a much larger number than quiz starts (37) because many visitors will have scrolled past it without engaging. The figures that count unique individuals — completions (17 unique users) and starts (34 unique users) — are the cleanest and most meaningful numbers in this report.
