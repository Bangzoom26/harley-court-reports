What This Month's Data Is Telling Us
The Recovery Is Real
Completions have more than doubled from July and the completion rate is back up to 52% — close to June's peak of 54%. We predicted a bounce back as summer wound down, and that's exactly what's happening. Impressions at 262 are the highest across all five months, which tells us the quiz is being seen more than ever even as overall site behaviour normalises after the holidays.
Lines Are Back — And Longevity With Them
Lines and wrinkles have returned to the top concern after being displaced through June and July, and longevity of results is firmly back as the number one priority. Karen, this is your classic anti-wrinkle audience re-engaging as summer ends. These visitors know what they want and they're quality-led — exactly the profile that converts well into consultations.
Experienced Clients Are Dominating
The "some experience" group has reached its highest level across all five months at 10 interactions, while first-time visitors remain low at just 2. This month's quiz audience is largely made up of people who have had treatments before and are thinking about their next one — a high-value, lower-barrier-to-convert audience. Worth bearing in mind when planning your September communications.
Value for Money Has Settled Back Down
After rising in July, value for money has dropped back to just 2 interactions this month — its lowest point across the entire five months. This suggests the content you put out last month addressing your 50s+ audience's perception of value may have done its job. The concern is less prominent, which is a positive signal that your messaging is landing.
Missed Opportunities — Where the Data Points to a Gap
Three things worth acting on this month
First-time visitors have gone quiet — and that's worth addressing. New-to-treatments visitors have been low for two months in a row (2 in July, 2 in August). This is the audience that represents true new business growth for Harley Court, and they've been harder to reach through summer. As autumn begins, a blog post or Google My Business post specifically welcoming first-timers — reassuring, jargon-free, focused on what a first consultation looks like — could help bring that number back up. The quiz is perfectly placed to guide them, but they need to feel confident enough to start it.
Skin quality is holding steady but there's no obvious content targeting it. Skin quality has appeared consistently in your top four concerns since July — but it tends to get less attention in aesthetic clinic content than injectables. A blog post focused on skin texture, skin boosters or pre-autumn skin prep would speak directly to a segment of your audience that's already showing up in your quiz data month after month. Title suggestion: "Why Autumn Is the Best Time to Start Looking After Your Skin."
The 40s age group is quietly growing and not being spoken to directly. Your 40s visitors have reappeared this month (3 interactions) after being almost absent in July. Across the full five months, they've been a consistent secondary audience behind the 50s+. This group tends to be earlier in their treatment journey — often thinking about prevention as much as correction — and they respond well to content about maintaining results and starting early. A newsletter section or blog post aimed squarely at this age group could open up a segment that's already finding you.
Marketing Recommendations — September Actions
September is historically one of the strongest months for aesthetic bookings. Your quiz data, your impression numbers, and the returning appetite of your audience are all pointing in the same direction. Here are four specific actions worth taking this month, matched to the channels you use.
Blog
Publish a first-timer's guide to aesthetic treatments
First-time visitors have been quiet for two months. A reassuring, plain-English blog post about what happens at a first consultation at Harley Court — who Karen is, what to expect, how decisions are made together — would speak directly to the hesitant new visitor. It also gives the quiz result page more credibility as a next step for someone who's never been before. Suggested title: "Thinking About Aesthetic Treatments for the First Time? Here's What to Expect at Harley Court."
Newsletter
Lead with an autumn availability message
Your September newsletter is one of the most valuable sends of the year. Your experienced-client audience is back, they're ready to book (7 this month said so), and they want longevity and natural results. A newsletter that opens with autumn availability, references Karen's expertise, and reminds clients that now is the ideal time to plan their next treatment — before the Christmas rush — will land at exactly the right moment.
Blog
Write about skin quality and autumn skin prep
Skin quality has been a top-four concern for three months running. September is the natural moment to publish content around skin rejuvenation — post-summer skin repair, skin boosters, and preparing skin for the winter months ahead. This is also a lower-barrier conversation than injectables for first-time visitors, which makes it a useful bridge piece. Suggested title: "Summer Is Over — Here's How to Give Your Skin a September Reset."
Google My Business
Post about autumn appointments with a direct call to action
A Google My Business post this week — simple, confident, direct — telling people that autumn appointments are open and linking to Karen's booking page or contact form will catch people who are searching locally and already in the mindset of booking. Keep it short: a line about the season, a line about availability, a link. Your Search Console data shows people are finding Harley Court through local searches — this is how you convert that visibility into action.
A note on the figures: Google Analytics records every individual interaction with the quiz, so if a visitor engaged across multiple sessions or revisited questions, each tap registers separately. Impressions reflect how many times the quiz was displayed across your site — always higher than starts, as many visitors will scroll past without engaging. The cleanest unique-user figures this month are completions (13 unique users) and starts (21 unique users).
