What This Means for Your Business
Volume Loss Is Having a Moment
Volume loss jumped from 6 interactions last month to 11 this month — nearly double. Karen, this suggests a growing segment of your audience is thinking about fillers and facial rejuvenation. It may be worth ensuring this treatment features prominently in your upcoming content and consultations.
Your Audience Wants to Look Like Themselves
Natural appearance has overtaken longevity as the number one priority this month, chosen by 11 visitors. This is a meaningful shift. The people visiting your site aren't looking for dramatic results — they want subtle, skilful work. Leading with that in your messaging will resonate.
More Experienced Clients Are Engaging
First-time visitors dropped from 12 to 5 this month, while those with some or regular treatment experience increased. This could reflect your newsletter audience — existing clients who know what they want and are considering their next treatment. Both audiences matter, and the quiz is serving both.
The 50s+ Audience Is Growing
Your over-50s audience increased from 8 to 13 visitors this month and is now even more dominant. This is your core demographic cementing itself. If you're creating content, running social ads, or planning seasonal promotions, this is the audience to speak directly to.
A note on the figures: Google Analytics records every individual interaction with the quiz, so if a visitor started it more than once, went back to change an answer, or engaged with it across multiple sessions, each tap registers separately. This is why total answers captured can appear high relative to the number of starters. The figures that count unique individuals — completions (14) and unique users (18) — are the cleanest and most meaningful numbers in this report. The new quiz impressions figure (26 tracked impressions, 24 button clicks) is a welcome addition to the data this month and will help build a clearer picture of the full visitor funnel going forward.
